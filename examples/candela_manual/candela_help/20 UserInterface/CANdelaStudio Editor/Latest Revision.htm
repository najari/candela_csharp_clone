Latest Revision

CANdelaStudio provides inspection and direct edition of the latest Revision in the "Latest Revision" window.

Position and Opening

The "Latest Revision" window is located below the work area but closed as a default.

To open the "Latest Revision" window, click "Latest Revision":

Closed Latest Revision at the Window Bottom

The window automatically closes after some time. Click the pin at top-right to keep it open:

Pin at Top Right of Latest Revision

You can also use the button in the ribbon tab "View" [MENU-R620] to open or close the window.

Contents

The window shows version, date and modification (text) of the latest revision.

The field "context" shows whether the revision history is on document level or local to a variant.

Operations

The button "Edit" opens the revision dialog.
