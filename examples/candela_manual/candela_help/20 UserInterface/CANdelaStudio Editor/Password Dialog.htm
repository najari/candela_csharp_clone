Passwort Dialog

If a CANdela document or template is password protected, CANdelaStudio prompts for the password on opening the document or template.

Protection for Standard View

With password protection for Standard View, you may either...

  • enter the password and press [OK] to open in Standard View, or
  • leave the password empty and press [OK] to open the document read-only, or
  • press [Cancel] to stop.
