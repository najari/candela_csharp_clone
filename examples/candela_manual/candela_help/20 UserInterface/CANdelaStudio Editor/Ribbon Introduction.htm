Ribbon Introduction

CANdelaStudio uses a ribbon instead of a traditional main menu since version 10 (2018-07).

A ribbon helps "finding commands quickly and confidently" (Microsoft about ribbon design).

CANdelaStudio Ribbon with Tab Home

Parts of a Ribbon

[RB-R020 Parts of a Ribbon]

  • Quick Access Toolbar on top
  • Application button "File": most left, opens the backstage, typically for file-related commands
  • Tabs (right to "File"): for commands that affect the content of the window (i.e. of the CANdela document)
  • Group: a part within a tab with a title at the bottom
  • Key tips: press the Alt key, keytips are displayed (a character to type for directly selecting a ribbon tab)

Minimize/Expand the Ribbon

[RB-R050 Minimize/Expand the Ribbon]

As a default, the ribbon is shown always.

[RB-R050-min] You can minimize it (show only the tab headers) by clicking the small up arrow at bottom right of the ribbon.

Button to Minimize the Ribbon

After minimizing the ribbon, ...

  • [RB-R050-tmp] when you click a Ribbon Tab header (like "Document"), the ribbon will temporarily expand and close again as soon as you select a command;
  • [RB-R050-exp] to expand the ribbon (show permanently) again, press the pin at bottom right of the ribbon:

Pin to Expand the Ribbon

[RB-R050-kb] Via keyboard, you can show/hide the ribbon by pressing Ctrl F1.

[RB-R050-ctx] You can also minimize/expand the ribbon via the context menu available for the ribbon buttons.

CANdelaStudio Navigation Area

[RB-R060] At top right, CANdelaStudio shows the navigation area: controls for navigation, and to open the online help.
