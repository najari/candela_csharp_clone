DTC Property Page

DTC Property Page

Navigation

Here you select the DTC that you wish to inspect or edit. Use the combo-box or click buttons [Up] or [Down] (right to the combo-box) to navigate through the list of DTCs.

Creation

Enter the DTC value in the edit control, then click [New DTC] to create the DTC.
