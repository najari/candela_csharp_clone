Icons (Service)

This chapter describes icons (symbols) for a service, used in the right side of a diagnostic instance.

These icons are shown on a button, between the service name (e.g. "Read") and the name of the Protocol Service (e.g. "($22) ReadDataByIdentifier"). The button opens the property dialog of the service.

Icon Service Condition
The service may be executed: physically ("P") and functionally ("F") (addressing)
The service cannot be executed directly in a test system (see property dialog, page "Authorization"; corresponds with the ODX property "IS-EXECUTABLE")
