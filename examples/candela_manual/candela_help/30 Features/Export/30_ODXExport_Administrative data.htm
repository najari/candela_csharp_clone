Export of LogMessages to File

[ODXE-R1030-Log]

With CANdela-Studio 5.5 it is possible to export the generated warnings during OdxExport to the Odx-File. The generated warnings are added to the Comment at the ODX-root-Element.

SDGs

SDGs are used to document manufacturer specific information within the ODX-data for which no specific data model element is defined by the standard. A similar mechanism exists in CANdela documents as well. Here Attributes are used to document process specific information.

Now, a generic mechanism is present to export CANdela Attributes as SDGs in ODX. CANdela Attributes of the category "SDG" are exported as SDGs in ODX with the following structure:

<SDG>
<SD SI="Attribute Name">Attribute Value</SD>
<SD SI="Attribute Name2">Attribute Value2</SD>
</SDG>

Depending on the location of the attribute, it is exported at specific locations in the ODX data.
