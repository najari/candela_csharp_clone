Romanization

Use-Case

When CANdelaStudio automatically generates a qualifier from a name, it replaces characters that are not in the restricted qualifier character set, e.g. “ä” by “ae”.

Character sets supported

Besides the Western European character sets (Latin alphabet with numbers and special characters), CANdelaStudio also support Eastern European character sets (Greek and Cyrillic alphabets), Asian character sets and pictograms (for example Chinese, Korean, Japanese) as well as Japanese syllabic alphabets. This means that you can also use these characters to enter names and descriptions.

However, since qualifiers may only contain ASCII characters, CANdelaStudio also includes functionality for converting Eastern European and Japanese characters into the Latin alphabet. This functionality is called „Romanization” and is used in the automatic generation of a qualifier from the name.

The Romanization function in CANdelaStudio currently supports the following character sets (see http://www.unicode.org/charts/):

  • Basic Latin
  • Latin 1 Supplement
  • Latin Extended-A
  • Latin Extended-B
  • Greek and Coptic
  • Cyrillic
  • Hiragana
  • Katakana

Characters from other Unicode character sets are converted to underscores „_” by the Romanization function. The same applies for punctuation marks and graphical or unprintable special characters. You can still use the other Unicode character sets for names, but then you must create the qualifier yourself.

How CANdelaStudio „Romanization” works

When you enter name, CANdelaStudio checks each individual character in the name, regardless of whether it is a letter or a graphical symbol.

If a character is in one of the character sets listed above, CANdelaStudio converts it to a Latin letter. Depending on the transcription scheme used for the character, the result may be a single Latin letter or even an entire Latin syllable.

Example

In order to transcribe Japanese words in Latin letters, the Hepburn transcription is generally used. This transcription scheme is also used by CANdelaStudio. In this scheme, individual Hiragana and Katakana syllables are represented in Latin letters based on their pronunciation:

Katakana: ト ラ ン ス ポ ― ト レ イ ヤ ―

Transcription: to ra n su po o to re i ya a

Accordingly, when the name „トランスポートレイヤー” is entered, CANdelaStudio generates the qualifier „toransupootoreiyaa”.
