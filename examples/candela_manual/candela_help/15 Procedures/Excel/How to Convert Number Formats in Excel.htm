How to Convert Number Formats in Excel

Use-case

You may have DTCs in Excel in the wrong format, say in hexadecimal, but need them in decimal format.

Solution

Convert a Number Using Formula in Excel

  • assume cell A1 contains 0x41
  • in cell B1 enter: =HEXINDEC(REPLACE(A1;1;2;""))

Excel: Number Conversion Formula

  • Note: for some languages, you must use non-English function names , like:
    • German HEXINDEZ
    • French HEXDEC
    • Spanish HEX.A.DEC
  • REPLACE(A1;1;2;"") removes the leading 0x,
  • HEXINDEC(...) converts the remaining hexadecimal number to a decimal number.
  • When you press ENTER, Excel evaluates the formula and shows decimal 65.

Convert an Entire Column of Values

  • Enter the formula in the first row, as described above;
  • leave editing of the cell
  • ensure the cell has focus
    • Excel shows a rectangle with a grip (dot) at bottom right

Excel: Number Conversion Formula Evaluated, showing Grip

  • with left mouse, drag down the dot over the relevant rows
  • Excel...
    • enters the formula in all cells, automatically adapting cell references like A1 to A2 in row 2,
    • evaluates all formulas and
    • shows the results:

Excel: Entire Column Converted

Transfer Converted Values to the Original Column

  • select the involved cell values of column 2, copy them;
  • set the focus into the first cell of column 1;
  • click context menu Paste Contents, Values.
  • You may need to delete column 2, finally.
